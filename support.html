Frequently asked questions

Where is my data stored? Only on your iPhone. Selfstock reads Apple Health, calculates your index on the device, and never uploads anything. There is no account and no server.

Selfstock shows no data. What can I do? Check that Selfstock may read your health data: Settings › Apps › Health › Data Access & Devices › Selfstock. iOS doesn't tell apps whether access was turned off, so the app can't tell the difference between "no access" and "no data yet".

How do I turn notifications on or off? In the app: Profile › Notifications. Or in Settings › Notifications › Selfstock.

What is a trading halt? A day you mark as sick, travelling or similar. The index doesn't move on a halted day. You can halt today and the past 2 days, up to 7 days a month.

How do I delete my data? Delete the app. Everything Selfstock stored (cached daily values, focus sessions, halts, weekly recaps, settings) is removed with it. Your data in Apple Health stays in Apple Health.

Is there a friends leaderboard? Not yet. If it's added, it will be optional, and the privacy policy will be updated before it's released.

常见问题

我的数据存在哪里？ 只存在你的 iPhone 上。Selfstock 读取 Apple 健康，在设备上计算指数，不上传任何数据。没有账号，也没有服务器。

Selfstock 没有显示数据怎么办？ 请确认 Selfstock 可以读取你的健康数据：设置 › App › 健康 › 数据访问与设备 › Selfstock。iOS 不会告诉 app 访问是否被关闭，所以 app 无法区分"没有权限"和"还没有数据"。

怎么开启或关闭通知？ 在 app 里：Profile › Notifications；或在 设置 › 通知 › Selfstock。

什么是停牌？ 生病、旅行等日子可以标记为停牌，这一天指数不变。可以停牌今天和过去 2 天，每月最多 7 天。

怎么删除我的数据？ 删除 app 即可。Selfstock 保存的全部内容（每日数值缓存、专注时段、停牌、每周回顾、设置）都会一起删除。Apple 健康里的数据仍保留在 Apple 健康中。

有好友排行榜吗？ 还没有。如果以后加入，它将是可选功能，并会在发布前更新隐私政策。
