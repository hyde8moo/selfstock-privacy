Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 26, 2026 · Last updated: October 30, 2026 (section 6)

Selfstock ("the app") turns your Apple Health data into a personal index. This policy explains what data the app uses and what happens to it. In short: everything is calculated on your iPhone, your health data is never uploaded, and we don't collect any data about you. If you choose to join a friends leaderboard, a small summary of your index is shared with that group through your own iCloud (section 6).

1. Health data the app reads

With your permission, the app reads these Apple Health (HealthKit) data types:

  • Steps
  • Walking + running distance
  • Active energy
  • Exercise minutes
  • Flights climbed
  • Workouts (start and end times)
  • Sleep (asleep and in-bed times)
  • Resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (SDNN)
  • Mindful Minutes, including the name of the app that recorded them (for example a focus app)

You choose which types to share in the system permission sheet, and you can change this at any time in Settings › Apps › Health › Data Access & Devices › Selfstock. The app does not write any data to Apple Health.

2. How the data is used

  • The app uses the data only to calculate your personal index, its components (Movement, Rest, Focus), the reasons behind its daily moves, and your weekly recaps.
  • All calculation happens on your iPhone. Health data is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Health data is not used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and it is never sold or shared.

3. Data the app keeps on your iPhone

The app stores the following on your device only:

  • A cache of the daily values read from Apple Health, so the app opens quickly.
  • Focus sessions you time or enter in the app.
  • Days you mark as trading halts, and the optional reason.
  • Your weekly recaps.
  • Your ticker and avatar. You can set a ticker and an avatar; they are stored only on your device. The avatar is chosen with the system photo picker, which gives the app only the photo you pick, not access to your photo library; the app keeps a small square copy of it without location or other metadata. The camera is never used.
  • Your settings (appearance, language, up/down colors, notification settings, how each Mindful Minutes app is classified).

None of this is uploaded (the friends leaderboard shares only what section 6 lists). There is no server of ours, because the app has none. Deleting the app deletes all of this data. Focus sessions recorded by other apps in Apple Health stay in Apple Health.

4. What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No data collection: the app makes no network requests to us or to third parties. The optional friends leaderboard uses only Apple's iCloud (section 6).

5. Notifications

If you turn them on, the app schedules local notifications on your iPhone: a daily reminder in the evening and a weekly recap reminder on Monday mornings. They are created on the device; no notification service or server is involved, and they contain no health numbers. You can turn them off in the app (Profile › Settings › Notifications) or in Settings › Notifications › Selfstock.

6. Friends leaderboard (optional)

The friends leaderboard compares how much each member's index moved. It is entirely optional.

  • Only if you choose to. Nothing is shared until you create a group or accept an invitation to one, after a screen that shows what will be shared.
  • What the group sees: your ticker (the code you chose, such as $HYDE); your index's daily change for each of the last 90 closed days; whether your index currently meets the ranking requirements (yes / no); when you last updated; and the app version.
  • What is never shared: any health data (steps, sleep, heart rate, Mindful Minutes or any other sample), the values of the Movement, Rest and Focus dimensions, your avatar, and which requirements you don't meet.
  • Where it is stored: in iCloud, using Apple's CloudKit sharing. A group lives in the iCloud account of the person who created it and is shared only with its members. Your ticker and daily changes are stored as CloudKit encrypted fields, which only group members' devices can read; we (the developer) cannot read them.
  • What iCloud shows: this uses Apple's own iCloud sharing, so a group's creator (and, in the system's share settings, other members) can see the iCloud name of the people in the group.
  • Pausing, leaving, deleting: in Settings › Friends leaderboard you can pause sharing (nothing new is uploaded; the group keeps your last data), and delete all your leaderboard data (your data is removed from every group, you leave them, and groups you created are deleted). On a group's page you can leave a group, and a creator can remove members or delete the group. Your own index and records on your iPhone are not affected.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them (it collects no information from anyone).

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change is released.

9. Contact

隐私政策（中文）

The English version above is the official text; this is a translation.

生效日期： 2026 年 10 月 26 日 · 最后更新： 2026 年 10 月 30 日（第 6 节）

Selfstock（以下简称"本应用"）把你的 Apple 健康数据变成一个个人指数。本政策说明本应用使用哪些数据，以及这些数据如何处理。简单来说：所有计算都在你的 iPhone 上完成，健康数据从不上传，我们不收集任何关于你的数据。如果你主动加入好友排行榜，指数的一份简要数据会通过你自己的 iCloud 分享给小组成员（第 6 节）。

1. 本应用读取的健康数据

在你授权后，本应用读取以下 Apple 健康（HealthKit）数据类型：

  • 步数
  • 步行 + 跑步距离
  • 活动能量
  • 锻炼分钟数
  • 已爬楼层
  • 体能训练（开始和结束时间）
  • 睡眠（睡着和在床时间）
  • 静息心率
  • 心率变异性（SDNN）
  • 正念分钟数，包括记录它的 app 名称（例如专注类 app）

你可以在系统授权页面选择共享哪些类型，也可以随时在 设置 › App › 健康 › 数据访问与设备 › Selfstock 中更改。本应用不会向 Apple 健康写入任何数据。

2. 数据的用途

  • 本应用只用这些数据计算你的个人指数、各组成部分（运动、休息、专注）、每日涨跌的原因和每周回顾。
  • 所有计算都在你的 iPhone 上进行。健康数据不会发送给我们，也不会发送给任何其他人。
  • 健康数据不会用于广告、营销或数据挖掘，也不会出售或共享。

3. 保存在你 iPhone 上的数据

本应用只在你的设备上保存以下内容：

  • 从 Apple 健康读取的每日数值的缓存，用于加快打开速度。
  • 你在本应用中计时或手动添加的专注时段。
  • 你标记为停牌的日子，以及可选的原因。
  • 你的每周回顾。
  • 你的代码和头像。你可以设置代码和头像，它们只保存在你的设备上。头像通过系统照片选择器选取，本应用只拿到你选中的那张照片，不能访问你的照片图库；本应用保存的是一张不含位置等元数据的小尺寸方形副本。本应用不会使用相机。
  • 你的设置（外观、语言、涨跌颜色、通知设置、各个正念 app 的分类）。

这些内容都不会上传（好友排行榜只分享第 6 节列出的内容），本应用没有任何服务器。删除本应用即删除以上全部数据。其他 app 写入 Apple 健康的正念记录仍保留在 Apple 健康中。

4. 我们不做的事

  • 没有账号，不需要登录。
  • 没有分析、追踪、崩溃报告或广告 SDK。
  • 不收集数据：本应用不会向我们或任何第三方发出网络请求。可选的好友排行榜只使用 Apple 的 iCloud（第 6 节）。

5. 通知

如果你开启通知，本应用会在你的 iPhone 上安排本地通知：每天晚上一条提醒，每周一早上一条周报提醒。通知在设备上生成，不经过任何通知服务或服务器，内容不含任何健康数字。你可以在本应用中（我 › 设置 › 通知；英文界面为 Profile › Settings › Notifications）或在 设置 › 通知 › Selfstock 中关闭。

6. 好友排行榜（可选）

好友排行榜比较各成员指数的涨幅，完全是可选功能。

  • 只有你主动选择才会分享。 只有在你创建小组，或者接受邀请加入小组之后才会分享；加入前会先显示一页，说明会分享什么。
  • 小组成员能看到的内容： 你的代码（你设置的，例如 $HYDE）；最近 90 个收盘日里每天的指数涨跌；你的指数目前是否满足上榜条件（是 / 否）；最近一次更新的时间；App 版本。
  • 从不分享的内容： 任何健康数据（步数、睡眠、心率、正念分钟数或其他任何样本）、运动 / 休息 / 专注各维度的数值、你的头像，以及你未满足的具体条件。
  • 存放在哪里： 存放在 iCloud，使用 Apple 的 CloudKit 共享功能。小组存放在创建者的 iCloud 账户里，只共享给小组成员。你的代码和每天的涨跌以 CloudKit 加密字段保存，只有小组成员的设备能读取；我们（开发者）也读不到。
  • iCloud 会显示什么： 这项功能使用 Apple 自带的 iCloud 共享，所以小组创建者（以及在系统的共享设置里，其他成员）能看到小组成员的 iCloud 名称。
  • 暂停、退出、删除： 在 设置 › 好友排行榜 里可以暂停分享（不再上传，小组里保留你暂停前的数据），也可以删除你的全部排行榜数据（从所有小组删除你的数据并退出，你创建的小组会整个删除）。在小组页面可以退出小组；创建者可以移除成员或删除小组。这些操作都不影响你 iPhone 上自己的指数和记录。

7. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集他们的任何信息（本应用不收集任何人的信息）。

8. 政策变更

如果本应用处理数据的方式发生变化，我们会在变更发布之前更新本政策及生效日期。

9. 联系方式

关于本政策的问题：hyde8mo@gmail.com
