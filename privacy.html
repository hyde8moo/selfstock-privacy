Privacy Policy

Effective date: [YYYY-MM-DD]

Selfstock ("the app") turns your Apple Health data into a personal index. This policy explains what data the app uses and what happens to it. In short: everything is calculated on your iPhone, nothing is uploaded, and we don't collect any data about you.

1. Health data the app reads

With your permission, the app reads these Apple Health (HealthKit) data types:

  • Steps
  • Walking + running distance
  • Active energy
  • Exercise minutes
  • Flights climbed
  • Workouts (start and end times)
  • Sleep (asleep and in-bed times)
  • Resting heart rate
  • Heart rate variability (SDNN)
  • Mindful Minutes, including the name of the app that recorded them (for example a focus app)

You choose which types to share in the system permission sheet, and you can change this at any time in Settings › Apps › Health › Data Access & Devices › Selfstock. The app does not write any data to Apple Health.

2. How the data is used

  • The app uses the data only to calculate your personal index, its components (Movement, Rest, Focus), the daily suggestions, and your weekly recaps.
  • All calculation happens on your iPhone. Health data is never sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Health data is not used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and it is never sold or shared.

3. Data the app keeps on your iPhone

The app stores the following on your device only:

  • A cache of the daily values read from Apple Health, so the app opens quickly.
  • Focus sessions you time or enter in the app.
  • Days you mark as trading halts, and the optional reason.
  • Your weekly recaps.
  • Your settings (display name, appearance, notification settings, how each Mindful Minutes app is classified).

None of this is uploaded. It is not included in any server, because the app has none. Deleting the app deletes all of this data. Focus sessions recorded by other apps in Apple Health stay in Apple Health.

4. What we don't do

  • No account or sign-in.
  • No analytics, tracking, crash reporting or advertising SDKs.
  • No data collection: the app makes no network requests to us or to third parties.

5. Notifications

If you turn them on, the app schedules local notifications on your iPhone: a daily reminder in the evening and a weekly recap reminder on Monday mornings. They are created on the device; no notification service or server is involved, and they contain no health numbers. You can turn them off in the app (Profile › Notifications) or in Settings › Notifications › Selfstock.

6. Friends leaderboard

A friends leaderboard is not available yet. If we add it, it will be optional, and we will update this policy before it is released, describing exactly what would be shared and with whom.

7. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect any information from them (it collects no information from anyone).

8. Changes to this policy

If the app's handling of data changes, we will update this policy and its effective date before the change is released.

隐私政策（中文）

The English version above is the official text; this is a translation.

生效日期： [YYYY-MM-DD]

Selfstock（以下简称"本应用"）把你的 Apple 健康数据变成一个个人指数。本政策说明本应用使用哪些数据，以及这些数据如何处理。简单来说：所有计算都在你的 iPhone 上完成，不上传任何数据，我们不收集任何关于你的数据。

1. 本应用读取的健康数据

在你授权后，本应用读取以下 Apple 健康（HealthKit）数据类型：

  • 步数
  • 步行 + 跑步距离
  • 活动能量
  • 锻炼分钟数
  • 已爬楼层
  • 体能训练（开始和结束时间）
  • 睡眠（睡着和在床时间）
  • 静息心率
  • 心率变异性（SDNN）
  • 正念分钟数，包括记录它的 app 名称（例如专注类 app）

你可以在系统授权页面选择共享哪些类型，也可以随时在 设置 › App › 健康 › 数据访问与设备 › Selfstock 中更改。本应用不会向 Apple 健康写入任何数据。

2. 数据的用途

  • 本应用只用这些数据计算你的个人指数、各组成部分（Movement、Rest、Focus）、每日建议和每周回顾。
  • 所有计算都在你的 iPhone 上进行。健康数据不会发送给我们，也不会发送给任何其他人。
  • 健康数据不会用于广告、营销或数据挖掘，也不会出售或共享。

3. 保存在你 iPhone 上的数据

本应用只在你的设备上保存以下内容：

  • 从 Apple 健康读取的每日数值的缓存，用于加快打开速度。
  • 你在本应用中计时或手动添加的专注时段。
  • 你标记为停牌的日子，以及可选的原因。
  • 你的每周回顾。
  • 你的设置（显示名称、外观、通知设置、各个正念 app 的分类）。

这些内容都不会上传，本应用没有任何服务器。删除本应用即删除以上全部数据。其他 app 写入 Apple 健康的正念记录仍保留在 Apple 健康中。

4. 我们不做的事

  • 没有账号，不需要登录。
  • 没有分析、追踪、崩溃报告或广告 SDK。
  • 不收集数据：本应用不会向我们或任何第三方发出网络请求。

5. 通知

如果你开启通知，本应用会在你的 iPhone 上安排本地通知：每天晚上一条提醒，每周一早上一条周报提醒。通知在设备上生成，不经过任何通知服务或服务器，内容不含任何健康数字。你可以在本应用中（Profile › Notifications）或在 设置 › 通知 › Selfstock 中关闭。

6. 好友排行榜

好友排行榜尚未提供。如果以后加入，它将是可选功能；在发布之前，我们会更新本政策，说明具体会共享什么、与谁共享。

7. 儿童

本应用不面向 13 岁以下儿童，也不会有意收集他们的任何信息（本应用不收集任何人的信息）。

8. 政策变更

如果本应用处理数据的方式发生变化，我们会在变更发布之前更新本政策及生效日期。
